Tire Size Calculator & Fitment

Privacy policy

nothing leaves your phone

Effective October 15, 2026

Tire Size Calculator & Fitment collects no data. Every calculation runs on your device, and the app works fully offline.

No account, no tracking

There’s no account and no sign-in. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Sizes you choose to save, and your unit preferences, are stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage. They’re deleted when you delete the app, and they aren’t backed up to any server we run.

The rating prompt

The app may show Apple’s standard prompt asking you to rate it. That prompt is handled by iOS, and the developer receives no personal data from it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
